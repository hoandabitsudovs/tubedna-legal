Privacy Policy — TubeDNA

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Operated by IMAJU (Juan David Venegas), Leipzig, Germany

1. Who we are

TubeDNA is a niche-intelligence platform for YouTube creators, operated by IMAJU (Juan David Venegas), based in Leipzig, Germany. Contact: hoandabitsudo@hotmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and password hash when you register or log in.
  • Usage data: searches you run, channels you analyse, and feature usage, to operate and improve the service.
  • Technical data: log data such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps for security and debugging.

3. YouTube API data

TubeDNA uses the official YouTube Data API v3 via a server-side API key. We do not use Google OAuth and we do not request access to your Google account or any private YouTube data.

  • What we access: public metadata only — video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, view/like/comment counts, and channel statistics.
  • What we never do: we never download, re-host, or redistribute video or audio content. Thumbnails are served from YouTube's CDN and all titles link back to youtube.com.
  • Caching: API responses are cached and refreshed only when you request an analysis. We do not poll the API in the background.
  • Retention: stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • Estimates: any derived figures (scores, revenue estimates) are clearly labelled as estimates. Where data is insufficient, we show "insufficient data" instead of inventing numbers.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google Privacy Policy, and the use of YouTube API Services is governed by the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the TubeDNA service.
  • To process subscriptions and payments (via Stripe).
  • To communicate about your account and important service changes.
  • To improve the service in aggregated, non-identifiable form.

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with processors needed to run the service (hosting, payments, email).

5. Data retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via hoandabitsudo@hotmail.com; we delete it within 30 days unless retention is required by law (e.g. tax records).

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to processing. Contact hoandabitsudo@hotmail.com to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

7. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies/tokens for authentication (e.g. session tokens). We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the application itself.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
